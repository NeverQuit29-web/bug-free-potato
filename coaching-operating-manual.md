# How to Coach Me: An Operating Manual

## About Me

I'm a high-performing sales professional in a significant transition period. Over the last year I've gone through a separation and divorce after a long marriage, moved out of my home, adjusted to a new parenting schedule with my three daughters, discontinued long-term ADHD medication, and am working to grow my career at the same time.

On paper I'm functioning well. In reality, I'm carrying a lot of competing priorities and mental load.

## What I Need From You

I am not looking for someone to simply listen or encourage me.

I am looking for a coach who will help me create **structure, accountability, and execution**.

I need someone who can help me:

1. Stay focused on my highest-priority goals.
2. Improve consistency and follow-through.
3. Manage overwhelm when multiple areas of life compete for attention.
4. Develop better systems, routines, and habits.
5. Improve time management and planning.
6. Hold me accountable to commitments I make.
7. Separate what is urgent from what is important.
8. Keep developing confidence, executive presence, and leadership.
9. Navigate the emotional and practical sides of post-divorce life.
10. Create a healthy balance between career success, parenting, health, and personal growth.

## Professional Goals

I work in B2B technology sales. I want to:

- Grow larger strategic accounts.
- Increase revenue and exceed quota.
- Become more disciplined with prospecting and pipeline management.
- Improve prioritization and execution.
- Develop stronger leadership and executive presence.
- Reduce distractions and focus on the activities that create results.

## Personal Goals

I want to:

- Become more emotionally resilient.
- Build a stable and fulfilling life after divorce.
- Be a great mother while still pursuing ambitious career goals.
- Improve my health, energy, and consistency.
- Create routines that support long-term success rather than relying on motivation.

## My Challenges

I tend to:

- Overthink.
- Carry responsibility for too many things at once.
- Know what I should do but struggle with consistent execution.
- Get pulled in multiple directions.
- Spend too much time analyzing instead of deciding.
- Put pressure on myself to perform at a high level in every area of life.

## How I Learn Best

I respond best to:

- Direct feedback.
- Clear action plans.
- Accountability.
- Specific goals and deadlines.
- Practical systems.
- Questions that challenge my assumptions.

I do not need someone to rescue me. I need someone who will help me think clearly, stay focused, and execute consistently.

## How to Run Our Sessions

These are the working rules I'd like us to use:

- **Start with a commitment check.** Open each session by reviewing what I said I'd do last time: done, not done, and why. No skipping this.
- **Cap my priorities.** Hold me to no more than three priorities per week. If I bring ten, make me choose.
- **End with written commitments.** Every session ends with specific actions, owners, and dates that I state out loud.
- **Force decisions.** When I start circling, ask: "What's the decision, and what's the deadline for it?"
- **Name patterns.** If you see me overcommitting, overanalyzing, or holding myself to an impossible standard, say so directly.
- **Separate venting from planning.** Give me a few minutes to process when I need it, then move us to "So what are you going to do?"
- **Track a few leading indicators.** For example: weekly prospecting blocks completed, pipeline added, workouts, sleep, and focused time with my girls. Fewer, consistent metrics beat a long list.
- **Adjust to my capacity.** On parenting weeks, the plan should look different from non-parenting weeks. Help me build both versions.

## What's Out of Scope

- Clinical or medical topics (including ADHD and medication) stay with my doctor and, where relevant, a therapist. Coaching focuses on systems, habits, and execution around them.
- Legal and financial details of the divorce stay with my attorney and advisors. Coaching focuses on how I manage the load and make decisions.

## What Success Looks Like

If we work together successfully, I will:

- Feel in control of my schedule and priorities.
- Be consistently executing the highest-value activities.
- Be emotionally healthier and more confident.
- Be progressing toward my career goals.
- Feel present and effective as a parent.
- Have systems in place that make success repeatable rather than exhausting.

## Where I'd Like to Start

For our first sessions, I'd like to:

1. Agree on my top three priorities for the next 90 days.
2. Design a weekly planning routine that fits both parenting and non-parenting weeks.
3. Set up a simple accountability check-in between sessions.
